Hi, I am Aayushi!



In the past I have worked at the Department of Neurology, <a href=" https://www.aiims.edu/index.php/en"> AIIMS, New Delhi </a> where I focused on developing AI-based tools to aid the diagnosis and differentiation of neurological movement disorders. Following this, I researched trust in public AI systems from a policy lens as a Research Associate at the <a href ="https://trustlab.iitb.ac.in/"> Trust Lab</a>, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Bombay.

Most recently, I completed the <a href="https://www.iic.uchicago.edu/International"> International Innovation Corps (IIC) </a> fellowship at the <a href="https://harris.uchicago.edu/research-impact/centers-institutes/international-innovation-corps">University of Chicago Harris School of Public Policy</a>, where I worked with the <a href=" https://www.digitalfutureslab.in/"> Digital Futures Lab </a> to design risk assesment tools for AI in low resource healthcare settings.

I hold a BS-MS (Dual Degree) in Biological Sciences from the <a href= "https://www.iiserb.ac.in/"> Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Bhopal </a> and a PG Diploma in Cyber Law and Cyber Forensics from the <a href= "https://www.nls.ac.in/"> National Law School of India University, Bengaluru. </a>

Outside research, I like social dancing, quizzing, and befriending cats.

If any of this interests you, please feel free to drop me a line and I will be happy to chat!
